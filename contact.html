Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for a loan application?

The required documents vary depending on the loan type. Generally, we need proof of identity, address, income, and property documents (if applicable). Our team will guide you through the specific requirements based on your loan type.

How long does the loan approval process take?

Our quick approval process typically takes 3-7 business days for most loan types. In some cases, we can provide instant approvals for eligible customers.

Can I prepay my loan without penalty?

Yes, we offer flexible prepayment options for most of our loan products without any penalty. Please check with our team regarding specific terms for your loan type.

What are the current interest rates?

Our interest rates are competitive and vary based on the loan type, loan amount, and tenure. Contact our team or use our EMI calculator to get an estimate of your interest rates.

Do you provide loans to self-employed individuals?

Yes! We specialize in providing tailored loan solutions for self-employed professionals, particularly CA's and Doctors. We understand your unique income structure and offer flexible documentation.

Can I apply for a loan online?

Absolutely! You can start your application through our website or contact us directly. Our team will guide you through the entire process.
